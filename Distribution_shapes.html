📚 Distribution Shape Concepts

📈 Normal Distribution

Bell-shaped, symmetric distribution where mean = median = mode.
Mean = Median = Mode
Example: Heights of Canadian adults, IQ scores, measurement errors
NORMAL DISTRIBUTION
Symmetric Bell Shape
Peak in center, equal tails

📈 Positively Skewed (Right-Skewed)

Distribution with a long tail extending to the right (higher values).
Mode < Median < Mean
Example: Canadian household incomes, housing prices, age at retirement
POSITIVELY SKEWED
Right-Skewed Distribution
Tail extends right → (higher values)

📈 Negatively Skewed (Left-Skewed)

Distribution with a long tail extending to the left (lower values).
Mean < Median < Mode
Example: Test scores on easy exams, age at death in developed countries
NEGATIVELY SKEWED
Left-Skewed Distribution
← Tail extends left (lower values)

📊 Mean-Median-Mode Relationships

The relative positions of central tendency measures reveal distribution shape.
Normal: Mean = Median = Mode
Right-Skewed: Mode < Median < Mean
Left-Skewed: Mean < Median < Mode
Example: If mean > median, expect positive skewness

🎯 Identifying Skewness

Look at tail direction and measure relationships to identify shape.
Tail Direction = Skewness Direction
Example: Long right tail = positively (right) skewed

📈 Real-World Implications

Distribution shape affects data interpretation and statistical analysis.
Shape → Analysis Method
Example: Skewed income data requires median over mean for typical income

⚡ Outliers and Skewness

Extreme values pull the mean toward the tail, creating skewness.
Extreme Values → Mean Shifts
Example: Few very high incomes pull mean above median

📏 Symmetry vs. Asymmetry

Normal distributions are symmetric; skewed distributions are asymmetric.
Symmetric ↔ Normal | Asymmetric ↔ Skewed
Example: Perfectly balanced vs. unbalanced data patterns

🔍 Statistical Analysis Impact

Distribution shape determines appropriate statistical tests and measures.
Normal → Parametric | Skewed → Non-parametric
Example: t-tests for normal data, rank tests for skewed data
